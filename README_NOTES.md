Reconstruction preformed as part of hte PRESTO Wrokshop May 2026.
This reconstructions includes records in Coralhydro2k and PAGES2k located in the North Atlantic region with at least 5 year resolution.
Records include corals, sclerosponges, marine sediment cores, and ice cores. 
The aim of this reconstruction was to look at temperatures, hurricanes, floods, and drought driven by temperature increases.
